Leadership in practice

How I work with systems and teams.

01 / ROOT CAUSE

Look for the why.

When something breaks, I want to understand why. Repeated failures are a cue to automate or improve the system, and incidents should lead to useful post-mortems.

02 / COACHING

Make the logic clear.

I use questions to help teammates reason through a design together. I favor readable, maintainable code over cleverness, and break complex problems into explainable parts.

03 / DELIVERY

Manage risk deliberately.

Production-like tests, feature flags, monitoring, alerts, and fallback plans help reduce risk. I weigh the effort against the value, deliver within agreed error levels, and align the stakeholders who own the outcome.
